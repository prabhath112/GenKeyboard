# GenKeyboard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-08

GenKeyboard is an Android keyboard app. This page explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

## What stays on your device

Everything you type is processed on your device. GenKeyboard does not log keystrokes, does not build a profile of you, and does not send your typing anywhere in the background. Word suggestions, spell checking, themes, clipboard history and all settings live only on your phone.

## What leaves your device, and only when you ask

GenKeyboard has optional AI writing tools (fix grammar, change tone, paraphrase, translate, reply, and similar). These only run when you open the AI panel and tap an action.

When you tap an action, the app sends:

- the text currently in the input field (or your selection),
- the action you chose (for example "fix grammar" or "professional tone"),
- a random device identifier generated by the app on first launch.

This is sent over HTTPS to the GenKeyboard API, a server we operate on Cloudflare Workers. The server forwards the text to a third-party AI provider to produce the result. Current providers are Google Gemini and OpenRouter. The provider's own privacy terms apply to that processing:

- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy

The result is returned to the app and shown to you. You decide whether to insert it.

## The random device identifier

The identifier is a random UUID. It is not your Android ID, advertising ID, phone number or account. We use it for one thing: a daily request limit per device so the free service is not abused. The counter is stored on the server for at most 48 hours and then expires. You can reset the identifier by clearing the app's data.

## What we do not collect

- No account, email, name or login.
- No contacts, location, photos or files.
- No analytics or advertising SDKs.
- No keystroke logging.

## Retention

Our server does not store the text you send or the AI result. It is processed in memory and discarded after the response is returned. Standard, short-lived Cloudflare request logs may exist for operational debugging.

## Passwords and sensitive fields

Android marks password fields, and GenKeyboard disables the AI tools in those fields. Please avoid using the AI tools on text you would not want an AI provider to process.

## Children

GenKeyboard is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same URL with an updated date.
