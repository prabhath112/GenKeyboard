Download for Android View source

Free during early access. Fair-use daily limit on AI actions.

How it works

Type as usual. Tap the sparkle in the top row, pick an action, review the result, then Replace or Discard. Nothing is inserted without you.

Writing tools

  • Fix grammar and spelling
  • Change tone: professional, casual, polite, funny and more
  • Paraphrase, shorten, expand, summarize
  • Translate to any language
  • Smart reply, or your own custom instruction

A real keyboard

Built on the open-source FlorisBoard engine: glide typing, suggestions that learn your words, themes, clipboard history, emoji, dozens of layouts and languages.

Privacy first

  • No account, no ads, no keystroke logging.
  • Text leaves your phone only when you tap an AI action, and only that text.
  • Not stored on our server. Password fields are excluded.
